Governed local inference

Verity gates requests submitted here against the selected agent's primary model and allowed purpose before contacting a loopback OpenClaw endpoint. Known unavailable or stale local connection health blocks this route until rechecked. A health response does not prove model readiness. Agents requiring fresh health refuse a never-checked connection. Older agents without that requirement remain legacy-allowed. This does not prevent direct calls to OpenClaw or same-user access outside Verity.
