Public Sector Product Line

three tiers — direction
Tier 1 — Public Sector Node Local-first triage for suspicious communications, URLs, email addresses, procurement/vendor requests, and incident artifacts.
Tier 2 — Agency Context Graph Adds people, roles, offices, approved vendors, domains, systems, procurement workflows, and sensitive mission workflows.
Tier 3 — Mission Evidence / Critical Infrastructure Ledger Adds incident evidence chains, cyber-physical signals, media authenticity, OT/ICS evidence, and cross-agency attribution workflows.

Tier 1 is what this prototype demonstrates. Tiers 2–3 are roadmap; no agency context graph or evidence ledger is loaded. Designed to support audit-ready workflows; no compliance or certification is claimed.
